Use this structure when you want to turn a real engineering task into a post
without drifting into a diary or a tutorial dump.

## 1. Background

What system were you working on? What constraint mattered most?

- latency
- quality
- maintainability
- operating cost
- delivery speed

## 2. Problem

Describe the specific failure mode.

- What symptom did users or engineers see?
- How did you measure it?
- Why was it worth fixing now?

## 3. Options Considered

List the main approaches and their tradeoffs.

1. Option A and why it was tempting
2. Option B and what risk it introduced
3. Option C and why you ultimately chose it or rejected it

## 4. Implementation

Keep this section lean:

- the few changes that mattered
- the one or two details that were not obvious
- the instrumentation or checks that kept the change safe

## 5. Result

Include evidence:

- before and after metrics
- what improved
- what stayed unresolved

## 6. Reflection

Close with one lesson that generalizes beyond the exact project.

That last section is where a post usually shifts from being a work log to being
something an interviewer can actually discuss with you.
